# Related Work and External Constraints

Documentation license: CC BY 4.0 -- see LICENSE for details.

This project must be read against the existing FRB literature. The directed-beam
model is not evaluated in a vacuum; it is a falsifiable geometric comparator
against active natural models.

For broad field context, use standard FRB reviews such as:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05878
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00159-022-00139-w

## Prior Technosignature Work

- Lingam and Loeb proposed FRBs from extragalactic light sails:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.01109

The novelty of this repository is therefore not "FRBs as directed energy" in
general. The novel test attempted here is the coupling between activity periods,
sub-burst drift, duty cycle, and possible aperture/beam-sweep geometry.

## Periodic Repeaters

- FRB 20180916B's 16.35 day activity cycle was reported by the CHIME/FRB
  Collaboration and is the primary benchmark for periodic-window tests:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.10275
- FRB 20121102A's reported long activity cycle is anchored to Rajwade et al.:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.03596
- The Second CHIME/FRB Catalog substantially expands the public repeater sample
  context: 4539 unique FRB events and 83 named repeater sources in the public
  CSV used by Stage 3. The larger catalog improves repeater demographics, but
  most downstream physics stages still require curated period, drift,
  polarization, RM, and propagation measurements:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09399
- FRB 20180916B remains the strongest periodic benchmark.
- FRB 20121102A has a reported long activity cycle, but window definitions are
  heterogeneous.
- FRB 20240209A should be treated as a candidate/possible periodic source:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11215

Population-level inference is not justified at n=2 robust plus one candidate.

## Natural Periodicity Comparators

- Binary-comb and binary-wind models remain direct natural comparators:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.08297
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.01920
- Precessing or ultra-long-period magnetar scenarios must be considered before
  treating periodic activity as anomalous:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04595
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.12509
- Magnetospheric FRB interpretations provide a counterpoint to engineered-beam
  framings:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.05093

## Chromatic Activity Windows

Recent work reports chromatic behavior in periodic repeater activity windows:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04609

This means achromaticity predictions must be stated carefully. A putative
source-frame achromatic signal would still require DM correction and propagation
modeling; observed activity-window chromaticity is a natural comparator that
must be addressed.

## Sub-Burst Drift and Propagation

- FRB 20121102A's complex time-frequency structure and representative drift
  values trace back to Hessels et al.:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.10748
- CHIME Catalog 1 and morphology papers are primary inputs/comparators for
  catalog usage and empirical drift laws:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04352
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04356
- CHIME/FRB baseband morphology shows the importance of high-resolution,
  coherently dedispersed data: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13215
- Sub-burst slope laws across repeaters provide natural/phenomenological
  comparators for drift-rate scalings: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11729

Any beam-sweep model must compare its functional form against these empirical
laws, not merely match one drift magnitude.

## Polarization and RVM Comparators

- FRB 20180916B polarization-angle behavior constrains precession but does not
  eliminate all rotational or magnetospheric models:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07651
- RVM-like PA swings have been reported in repeating FRBs:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00391

Therefore, flat PA should be treated as a constraint requiring model comparison,
not as a standalone falsification of natural models.

## Rotation Measure

- FRB 20180916B RM evolves stochastically and secularly:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12584
- FRB 20220529 and RM-periodicity work keep binary/wind-style natural
  comparators in play: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10463
- RM flare searches show RM variability is a population diagnostic:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20814

Stable or slowly varying RM can constrain some dense-wind geometries, but does
not by itself falsify all natural binary explanations.
